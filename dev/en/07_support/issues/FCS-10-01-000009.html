FCS-10-01-000009

ERROR

Message

Processing settings were not applied.

Why this happens

  • Applying processing settings rejected a numeric value, smoothing method, gaze replacement, profile-priority option, or clamp mode. The checks below are independent; correct the setting you changed.

How to resolve it

  1. Same-video weight must be between 1.0 and 10.0, inclusive; for example 5 is accepted.

  2. Gaze-freezing threshold must be between 0.0 and 1.0, inclusive; for example 0.5. Replacement must be Last detected or Default value.

  3. Profile-priority range must be between 0.0 and 2.0 seconds, inclusive; its exponent must be between 0.1 and 5.0, inclusive. For example, 1 second and exponent 1 are accepted. Select a priority mode offered for the current processing profile; availability depends on that profile.

  4. Smoothing method must be Smoothing, Peak, 1€ or Raw. For 1€, minimum cutoff must be a finite number greater than 0; beta must be finite and at least 0. The derivative cutoff is fixed internally at 1.

  5. Clamp mode must be No Clamp, Hard Clamp or Soft Clamp. Choose an option from the app rather than entering a translated label through automation.

  6. Correct the affected field in Processing Settings and apply again. For automation, use the field names and nesting returned by processing.settings rather than adding unsupported keys.
