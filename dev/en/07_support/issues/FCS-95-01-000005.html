FCS-95-01-000005

ERROR

Message

The session could not be exported.

Why this happens

  • Session export checks the destination, Actor and Character, selected media, and any replacement DCC scene before copying the session.

How to resolve it

  1. Use the matching check below, correct the export settings, then click Export again. The current session remains the source.

Choose an export destination and enter both Actor and Character. The exported session is created at destination/FCS/Actor/Character.
